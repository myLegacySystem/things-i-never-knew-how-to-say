# Editorial notes

These notes explain what was changed in *Things I Never Knew How to Say*, why, and what was deliberately left alone.

## The finished files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `things-i-never-knew-how-to-say_book_ready.docx` | The complete book: title page, contents, and 7 chapters in their final order. Trim size 5.5 × 8.5 in, set in EB Garamond with the regular and italic fonts embedded so it prints the same on any computer. |
| `things-i-never-knew-how-to-say_cover.jpg` | Front cover, 1725 × 2625 px at 300 DPI. That is 5.5 × 8.5 in plus a 0.125 in bleed on every side. |
| `things-i-never-knew-how-to-say_back.jpg` | Back cover, same size and style. |
| `_editing/chapters/*.md` | The edited text of each chapter, in plain text, so future changes are easy to make. |
| `_editing/build/` | Scripts that rebuild the DOCX and covers from those files (see the end of this document). |

Your original seven DOCX files have not been touched.

## 2. The chapter order

| # | Chapter | What it is | Why it goes here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **24 August — 10:46 a.m.** | Thought | It shows who you are before it shows who she is: friends, responsibility, your mom, wanting to be a good son and a good person, the overthinking, the waiting for one notification. She appears only as "her", someone whose replies you wait for. It ends on "Having a thousand things inside your heart and still getting up to do what needs to be done." That is the right opening note for the whole book. |
| 2 | **27 August** | Story | The same week. 24 August 2026 was a Monday and 27 August was a Thursday. You end Monday with "Yesterday, we talked. And somehow, it still didn't feel like enough." Two days later she asks if you are free. The reader meets her in a real scene early (the pink top, the dosa, the shared glass of watermelon juice, the chocolate on the bridge) and starts to care. |
| 3 | **The Flower I Keep Seeing** | Reflection | After a day with her, you describe who she is to you. This chapter introduces the plumeria, the tree that protects it, the rain and the sky. That imagery runs through the rest of the book. It ends with "I hope the flower still blooms. Even if it isn't in my garden." |
| 4 | **The Flower I Chose Not to Pick** | Context and memory | The middle of the book is where the reader learns what they have been wondering since Chapter 1, when you wrote "even if she wanted to say yes, maybe she still couldn't". Here they learn about the caste difference, her brother being one of your best friends, her parents, the age gap, her degree, and that you have already told her how you feel. It also tells how it all began: the trip, the car ride, the pool, the chicken. The two flower chapters sit side by side, so the metaphor grows from *seeing* the flower to *choosing not to pick it*. This also makes the "tree" in Chapter 3 make sense in hindsight. |
| 5 | **The Weight of Thinking About You** | Thought | Right after "if letting her bloom means walking away", the next chapter opens with "For the past two or three days, we haven't talked." It reads as the cost of that decision. This is the lowest point emotionally: the emptiness, "cut all the threads", the remote control for your brain. It is also the clearest statement of why you write. |
| 6 | **The Love I Could Never Explain** | Reflection | Grief for the imagined life, "my first true love", and gratitude. It sounds like an ending ("Maybe not the ending I wanted"), and that is why it is second to last. |
| 7 | **Will She Ever Understand?** | Thought | It opens with "There is something I have been thinking about while writing all of this." Its last lines look back over the whole book: "After everything I have written… After all the responsibilities waiting for me… After all the thoughts about my career, my family, my future…" After all the acceptance in Chapters 4 and 6, the heart is still waiting for one notification. It ends unresolved, the way the book itself is still unfinished, and it asks the question the title asks. |

The shape of the book: restlessness, then joy, then wonder, then the truth, then emptiness, then grief, then *still waiting*.

**The first and last chapters mirror each other.** Both are about checking the phone. Both have "Maybe. Maybe. Maybe." In the last chapter, "I use that word so much" lands because the reader has already seen it. Chapter 1 asks "Will these words ever make her understand my love?" and Chapter 7 is titled *Will She Ever Understand?*

**If you add the missing "I just want to be loved" piece,** I would put it near the end. A good spot is between *The Love I Could Never Explain* and *Will She Ever Understand?*, or straight after *The Weight of Thinking About You*. Its ending about your mom and dad would follow naturally from the passage about your mom in Chapter 5.

## 3. How I edited

The text was already clear and very much yours, so the edits are light: **12,322 words became 12,295, and 99% of the words are untouched.** I compared every chapter to your original word by word. Below is the complete list of changes. Nothing else changed.

### Chapter 1: 24 August — 10:46 a.m.
- "someone **whom** you wish would understand you" became "someone **who** you wish would understand you". *(grammar)*
- "And **later** I realize something" became "And **then** I realize something". *(the "later" read oddly in the present tense)*
- "But maybe **that is enough**." became "But maybe **enough is enough**." *(the original could be read as "what I have is enough", the opposite of what you meant)*
- "no one not even a single person can fully understand me" became "no one **—** not even a single person **—** can fully understand me". *(dashes lost when exporting from Google Docs)*

### Chapter 2: 27 August
- "go to a college to enquire about the fee structure and see the college where she was thinking of taking admission" became "go and see a college where she was thinking of taking admission, and enquire about the fee structure". *(removes the doubled "college")*
- "Then dosa arrived." became "Then **the** dosa arrived."
- Two commas added.
- **Paragraphs:** this is the most story-like chapter, so I grouped the plain logistics into short paragraphs: getting off the train, looking for an auto, looking for a café. Every emotional beat still sits on its own line.

### Chapter 3: The Flower I Keep Seeing
- Title changed from all caps to the same case as the other titles. No other changes.

### Chapter 4: The Flower I Chose Not to Pick
- **Removed the first line, "I think the same thing is happening with me."** It refers to something the reader never sees. It was probably a quote, a reel, or something you had just read. Without it, the chapter opens with "I love a girl, but I don't think she loves me back." **If you remember what it referred to and want it back, it is a one-line change.**
- "nothing **that I knew** would become so important" became "nothing **I thought** would become so important".
- "Then, at the pool, she was trying to get out. I held her hand and pulled her out of the swimming pool." became "Then, when she was trying to get out of the pool, I held her hand and pulled her out." *(removes the doubled "pool")*
- "After some time, we freshened up." became "Then we freshened up, and…". *("After some time" was used in the line just before it)*
- A lost dash restored before "small rocks, frogs".

### Chapter 5: The Weight of Thinking About You
- Four lost dashes restored.
- "behavior" became "behaviour", to match the British spelling used everywhere else (colour, favourite, travelled).

### Chapter 6: The Love I Could Never Explain
- "The way a mother can feel when her child is hurting without being told, I wish I could somehow make her feel the depth of what exists inside me." became two sentences: "A mother can feel when her child is hurting without being told. I wish I could somehow make her feel the depth of what exists inside me in that same way."
- **Removed two lines:** "Maybe in that world, we get to live the life I always dreamed about. The life I spent so much time imagining." A few lines earlier the same passage already says "Where we lived all those imaginary moments I created", so this was the one place where repetition weakened the moment.

### Chapter 7: Will She Ever Understand?
- "Will that wanting her period ever end?" became "Will that **period of wanting her** ever end?"
- "Will she wonder why this guy wants me so badly?" became "Will she wonder, *Why does this guy want me so badly?*" The question is in her imagined voice, so it is set in italics, the same way you used italics for imagined voices in Chapter 5 ("*How stupid is this guy?*").

### Across the whole book
- Straight quotes became curly quotes, "..." became "…", and your bold emphasis became italics. Bold looks loud on a printed page. The emphasis is still in the same places: *I love her*, *I know*, *she*, *What it feels like to love someone with everything you have*.

## 4. What I deliberately did NOT change

These might look like things an editor "should" fix. I left them because they are you.

- **All the meaningful repetition:** "Maybe. Maybe. Maybe.", "A lot. A lot. A lot.", the "Will I be a good son… friend… partner… father…" list, "the people closest to me" coming back again and again, and every "Every thought. Every…" list. They show how your mind loops, and the book needs that.
- **The trip memories told three times in Chapter 4.** First as a list ("A simple trip. A car ride. A few songs…"), then as "the girl who…", then as "I will carry…". It is a chapter about holding on to memories, so going back to them is the point.
- **The same thoughts in different chapters.** Hoping another man will be good to her (Chapters 1, 4, 6), "another life" (Chapters 4 and 6), "nobody will read this" (Chapters 1, 5, 7), cutting the threads and then killing the feeling (Chapters 5 and 7). In the new order these read as one mind coming back to the same fears, and some of them grow stronger each time.
- **Contradictions:**
  - "I just want one thing", followed by four things.
  - "Maybe that's not even love. Maybe that's just what it feels like when you're deeply in love."
  - "Is there someone who could love her more than me? I don't think so."
  - Hoping you'll find love one day, then "perhaps I don't want it to [come back]".
  - Chapter 6 accepting she didn't choose you, then Chapter 7 asking "What if, one day, she says yes?"
- **"I told her I was near the ticket counter and that I hadn't seen her"** in *27 August*, right after you wrote that you *had* seen her. It reads like a small nervous white lie, and it's lovely.
- **The ambiguous "her" in Chapter 5:** "I have never told my mom how much I love her… And maybe it is the same with her." It could mean your mom or the girl. I did not choose for you.
- **The dark lines:** "Sometimes I even wonder why I live", "Maybe I'll get into an accident", "healthy enough to attend my wedding. Or maybe even my funeral."
- **Your own words:** "bullshit", "typing this shit", "damn…", "crash out", "cringe", "abuse", "pakad", "taking admission", "achieve great heights", "the bright orange light hitting my retina".
- **Every small detail:**
  - Your glasses and blurry eyes in the morning.
  - The pink top and the pink shirt.
  - Her hair on your face.
  - The dosa, the watermelon juice, and the chocolate.
  - The single picture in your Drive.
  - The pakad held the wrong way round.
  - The white top and black skirt.
  - The cat near the parking lot.
  - The Radha and Krishna wallpaper.
  - 5:44 p.m. and 5:58 p.m.
- **The Instagram quote**, kept word for word as a quote, because it is someone else's words.
- **The titles.** All seven were already right. The two dates work as titles because, as you wrote, "I don't even know what to call this chapter."

## 5. Covers

**Front:** a single white plumeria on a muted, after-rain blue-grey, with very faint rain streaks like a window. It comes from the manuscript:
- "Whenever I think about her, I think about a flower. Plumeria."
- The flower that "falls from the tree".
- The rain you watch from the window.
- "She was like a warm light to my eyes."

It is the only bright thing on the cover. There is lots of empty space, a quiet serif title, and no couples, roses or hearts.

**Back:** the quote is word for word from *The Flower I Keep Seeing*:

> And if that day never comes…
> I hope the flower still blooms.
> Even if it isn't in my garden.

The description below it uses only things that are in the book:

> Most of this was written at a desk by a window, in the middle of an ordinary working day.
>
> By someone who replies within milliseconds and then waits. Who checks the phone each morning before even putting on glasses. Who reached the station fifteen minutes early. Who still has one picture from a trip saved in a Drive folder.
>
> It is about a girl, and a plumeria, and the rain. About overthinking every message. About family, responsibility, and wanting to be good to the people closest to you. About loving someone enough to let them bloom.
>
> It is not a story with an ending.
> It is the things he never knew how to say.

The bottom-right corner of the back cover is left empty in case a barcode or ISBN is added later. There are no reviews, logos or credentials of any kind.

## 7. Print notes

- The book opens with a title page, then contents, then Chapter One. The front matter and Chapter One each start on a right-hand page, so Word will show a blank left-hand page before them. That is normal for a printed book. Page numbers start at 1 on Chapter One.
- Margins are mirrored: the wider margin is always on the binding side.
- The covers include 0.125 in of bleed on every side for trimming. All text is well inside the safe area.
- The font, EB Garamond, is free under the SIL Open Font License, so embedding and printing it is allowed.

## 8. Rebuilding after changes

Edit the chapter files in `_editing/chapters/`. A blank line starts a new paragraph, `*text*` makes italics, and a line starting with `> ` is an indented quote. Then run:

```bash
python3 _editing/build/get_fonts.py fonts          # once: downloads the open-licensed fonts
node _editing/build/build_book.js _editing/chapters raw.docx "Optional Author Name"
python3 _editing/build/finish_docx.py raw.docx things-i-never-knew-how-to-say_book_ready.docx fonts/EBGaramond-400.ttf fonts/EBGaramond-400i.ttf
node _editing/build/covers.js fonts covers-out "Optional Author Name"
# then convert covers-out/front.png and back.png to the two JPGs
```
