# The Flower I Chose Not to Pick

I love a girl, but I don't think she loves me back.

Maybe I am seeing things differently because I am the one who feels all of this so deeply. Maybe for her, we are just friends. Maybe she doesn't think about me the way I think about her. And perhaps that is why, sometimes, it feels like she doesn't care about me.

There is also everything that stands between us.

My family has its expectations. She has her own family, her own dreams, and her own fears. She is three years younger than me. I have started working and building my life, while she is still completing her degree. In another world, maybe the timing would have been different. Maybe these things wouldn't feel so heavy.

But in this world, they do.

We come from different castes. Her brother is one of my best friends. She doesn't want to ruin that friendship. She doesn't want to disappoint her parents by marrying a boy from a lower caste. And I understand that. As much as it hurts me, I understand that she has her own life to protect, her own family to think about, and her own battles that I cannot fight for her.

Sometimes it feels like everything is against me.

Even the girl I love.

Not because she is cruel. Not because she has done something wrong. But because perhaps she simply cannot see me the way I see her.

And maybe that is why a thought has slowly started growing inside me.

Maybe loving her means letting her live her life without interfering with it.

There is a quote about loving a flower so much that, instead of picking it, you leave it where it is so it can continue to bloom. I think I finally understand what that means.

Sometimes, loving someone is not about having them.

Sometimes, it is about wanting them to be happy even when their happiness doesn't include you.

Maybe she is that flower for me.

And if I truly love her, perhaps I have to let her bloom.

I don't know if I will ever stop wishing that things could have been different. Maybe in another life, we would have found each other at the right age, at the right time, without families, caste, fear, or circumstances standing between us. Maybe in another life, we would have met when everything was easier.

I sometimes wish that next life exists.

I wish that in that life, she and I find each other at the perfect time.

But for now, I have these memories.

And I want to keep them with me until my last breath.

Because there was a trip.

A simple trip with friends.

And somehow, that trip became one of the most beautiful chapters of my life.

We had only met once before everything started changing between us. It was just a friends' trip, nothing I thought would become so important to me.

For the first one and a half hours, I was on a bike. Then I got into the car for the next one and a half hours.

And she was sitting beside me.

It wasn't for very long.

But for me, it was enough to become a memory I know I will carry for the rest of my life.

I was playing romantic songs.

We sang together.

We laughed.

We enjoyed that little moment.

It was ordinary for everyone else, probably. Just friends sitting together in a car.

But for me, something about having her beside me made that moment feel different.

We took a single picture together, and I still have it saved in my Drive.

Sometimes I look at that picture and wonder how something so small could mean so much to me.

Then we met on the terrace.

I panicked.

My heart was racing so badly that I left.

I don't even know why I did that.

Maybe I was nervous because suddenly she wasn't just another girl on the trip anymore. I was becoming too aware of her.

Then we met at the pool.

I was playing with a volleyball, but my eyes were on her.

Almost the entire time.

It felt like my eyes were constantly searching for her.

Where is she?

What is she doing?

Is she okay?

Is she having fun?

I don't know when it happened, but somehow my attention always found its way back to her.

She was like a warm light to my eyes.

I didn't want her to leave my eyesight.

I was constantly trying to impress her with whatever little volleyball skills I had.

Looking back, it is funny.

I don't even know if she noticed.

But I was trying.

Then, when she was trying to get out of the pool, I held her hand and pulled her out.

I will never forget that touch.

It was only a moment.

A simple touch.

But some moments don't need to last long to stay with you forever.

After that, we started playing on the wet ground where there was mud everywhere. Everyone was playing and having fun.

Someone dragged her into the mud too.

And I immediately became worried.

I kept watching her, looking around for little things that could hurt her — small rocks, frogs, anything that might be in the way.

I wanted to be the person who would reach her first if something happened.

Maybe she never knew that.

Maybe she never noticed.

But I was watching.

I always seemed to be watching.

Then we went into the kitchen.

We were cracking jokes with my friends, and she was there, laughing and teaching me and my friends how to fry chicken.

I mostly just watched her from the side.

Then it was my turn to fry the chicken.

And I fumbled so badly.

First, I held the pakad from the wrong side.

Then my already terrible cooking skills decided to betray me completely.

She and my friends kept laughing.

And while everyone was laughing at my cooking disaster, I was mostly looking at her.

Her laugh was what I noticed.

Her smile was what I remembered.

Even when I was the joke, I didn't really mind.

Because she was laughing.

Later, we went to play games.

She was about to sit beside me, but another girl came and sat there first.

I was quite angry.

I don't even know why something so small bothered me so much.

But then I felt like she was angry too.

And strangely, that made me happy.

Maybe she had wanted to sit beside me too.

Maybe.

Or maybe I was simply hoping she did.

I don't know.

But sometimes, when you love someone, even the smallest possibility becomes a whole story inside your head.

Then came the reels.

The dancing.

The laughter.

Everyone was enjoying the night together.

I am not a dancer, so I stood in the corner, mostly watching.

Watching her.

She was enjoying herself, laughing, dancing, being completely free in the moment.

And while I was watching her enjoy herself, somehow I started enjoying the night too.

Maybe happiness can be contagious when it comes from someone you love.

Eventually, it was time to sleep.

And somehow, even the next morning, my eyes started looking for her.

After some time, they found her.

And damn...

Even after waking up, she was beautiful.

There was something about seeing her in those ordinary moments that made her even more beautiful to me.

She wasn't trying to impress anyone.

She was just being herself.

And maybe that's what I loved most.

Then we freshened up, and I went to play badminton.

She was ready in a white top and black skirt.

She looked so beautiful that I wanted to stop playing and just look at her.

But she was busy with her sister's photoshoot, so I kept playing.

Then the rain started.

She changed her outfit and got into the pool again.

And once again, my eyes found her.

I kept looking at her.

I don't know how many times I did that during the entire trip.

Maybe too many.

But I couldn't help it.

As the trip was coming to an end, something inside me already knew that this might be the last time I would see her for a while.

Maybe weeks.

Maybe months.

Maybe much longer.

I knew I would remember this trip.

I knew I would remember the moments I had with her.

I just didn't know then how important those moments would become.

And perhaps that trip was lucky in a way.

Because after that, we started talking.

Eventually, I expressed my feelings.

I told her everything.

Things I had been keeping inside.

And somehow, that trip became the reason a conversation between us even started.

A simple trip.

A car ride.

A few songs.

A few laughs.

A pool.

A hand.

A little mud.

A terrible attempt at frying chicken.

A game.

A night of dancing.

A rainy morning.

A girl in a white top and black skirt.

And a boy who couldn't stop looking at her.

Those are the memories I have.

And lately, I have been feeling that maybe this is all coming to an end.

Maybe soon, we will have to separate.

Maybe life will take us in different directions.

Maybe I will become just another chapter in her life, while she remains one of the most important chapters in mine.

And it hurts.

It hurts because I didn't want this story to end here.

I wanted more moments.

More conversations.

More trips.

More songs.

More laughter.

More chances to sit beside her.

But perhaps love isn't always about getting more.

Perhaps sometimes love is simply being grateful that you got to experience something beautiful at all.

If she cannot choose me, I don't want to force her.

If her happiness lies somewhere else, I want her to be happy.

If her family matters more to her than being with me, I cannot hate her for that.

If she only sees me as a friend, then maybe I have to accept that too.

Because I don't want to pick the flower just because I love it.

I want to leave it where it is and let it bloom.

Maybe that is the hardest kind of love.

To love someone enough to let them live freely.

To care for them without demanding that they belong to you.

To keep the memories without trying to turn them into promises.

And maybe one day, I will look back at all of this and smile.

Maybe I will remember the girl who sat beside me in a car while romantic songs played.

The girl whose hand I held for only a moment.

The girl I watched in the pool.

The girl I worried about when she was playing in the mud.

The girl who laughed while I struggled to fry chicken.

The girl I kept looking for in every room.

The girl who somehow became the warm light my eyes were always searching for.

Maybe I will remember her and be grateful.

Grateful that, even if I couldn't keep her, I got to know what it felt like to love someone this deeply.

And if this really is where our story ends, then I hope she lives beautifully.

I hope she finishes her degree.

I hope she makes her parents proud.

I hope she finds happiness.

I hope she laughs the way she did on that trip.

I hope she finds someone who makes her feel safe, loved, and understood.

And maybe, somewhere in another life, at another time, when everything is finally right...

I hope our paths cross again.

Maybe there, I won't have to let the flower go.

But in this life, if letting her bloom means walking away, then perhaps that is what I have to do.

I will carry the memories.

I will carry that picture.

I will carry that car ride, those songs, those laughs, that touch, that trip, and everything I felt.

Because some people don't stay in our lives forever.

Sometimes they stay in our hearts instead.

And perhaps she will always be one of those people for me.
