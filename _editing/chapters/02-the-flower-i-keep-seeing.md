# The Flower I Keep Seeing

She is beautiful.

She is gorgeous.

And somehow, whenever I think about her, I think about a flower.

Plumeria.

I don't know why.

Maybe because she feels like one.

She is a happy soul. Whenever I talk to her, whenever I see her, my life automatically becomes a little more colourful. I started seeing things differently after she came into my life.

There is a different sparkle in her eyes.

It always tells me that she is genuinely excited about the things she loves.

She gets angry very quickly.

She gets upset very quickly.

She gets hurt immediately.

But she is always open to talking if something is bothering her.

She is hard to convince.

She listens.

She tries to understand.

Even when she doesn't seem to understand, she tries.

And maybe that's what makes her special.

I don't even know yet what she truly loves.

I don't know what truly makes her happy.

I hope one day I get to know.

And maybe, when I do, I'll be able to give her that happiness.

I don't know why I think like this.

I don't know why I think about her so much.

Why does every thought somehow find its way back to her?

Even when I'm looking at a bright blue sky, somehow it feels like I'm looking at her bright smile.

And when the sky isn't bright, when it's dark and raining, I still find her there.

There is a freshness in rain.

That feeling you get when you first step into it.

Everything feels reborn.

The trees.

The leaves.

The roads.

Everything that looked ordinary suddenly looks alive again.

And somehow, when I sit there watching the rain and thinking about her, I feel the same thing.

A fresh breath of air.

A feeling that something inside me has been reborn.

But sometimes the rain feels heavy too.

Because rain reminds me that one day, everything will end.

And I'll be alone by myself.

Sometimes too much rain destroys the things we were enjoying.

And I wonder if love can do the same.

Maybe one day, too much of this feeling will destroy something inside me.

But for now, let me enjoy this rain.

Let me enjoy this sky.

Let me take in the fragrance of the flowers.

Let me enjoy these things while I can.

Because I know things don't last forever.

Things end.

The bright sky eventually becomes a dark night.

The moon you once admired, the moon whose light you held inside your heart, eventually disappears.

The stars you once looked at thinking they would always be there eventually fade.

The rain you once enjoyed can become a flood.

And that flood can take away everything.

The lightning strikes.

The clouds close in.

And the sound of thunder feels like something inside you is bursting.

Maybe that's what I'm afraid of.

Not losing her today.

But losing the feeling that exists today.

The flower whose fragrance I enjoyed.

The flower I kept looking at.

The flower I dreamed of seeing bloom.

The flower I watched as if it belonged to me.

The flower I imagined taking care of.

And then one day, it falls from the tree.

It was held there for so long.

Protected by the tree.

Sheltered from the heavy rain.

Protected from the fast winds.

And then, somehow, the tree hands it over to someone else.

Maybe that flower falls for someone else.

Maybe the tree thinks I wasn't good enough.

Maybe it thinks I wouldn't have taken care of the flower the way it did.

Maybe someone else was always meant to hold it.

And maybe that's okay.

But I know one thing.

Even after all of that, I'll still worry about that flower.

I'll still wonder whether it is safe.

I'll still wonder whether it is happy.

I'll still worry about the bright sky that once added colours to my life.

I'll worry about the moon that was there when the night felt dark.

I'll remember the rain that once made my heart feel fresh and reborn.

Because once something becomes a part of you, I don't think you can simply stop caring about it.

And right now, these words, these feelings, everything I'm thinking about, even the things buried somewhere deep inside my subconscious...

They all lead back to her.

The little things she says that somehow make my day.

The emojis she sends while texting.

Her asking about my day.

Her asking whether I've had dinner.

Whether I've had lunch.

Whether I'm there.

And I'll always be there.

Even when I can't.

Even when I'm about to crash out.

Even when I feel like I'm falling apart.

Somewhere inside me, the thought of her will still be there.

The worry.

The feeling.

The trust.

It will always be there for her.

Because it's not about whether she loves me.

It's about the fact that *I love her.*

Maybe that's the difference.

I'm loving her even when she isn't there.

I'm loving her even when she doesn't love me back.

I'm loving her without knowing where this will end.

Because sometimes I don't feel like *I am choosing* to love her.

It feels like something inside me already decided.

Something that doesn't want to let go.

Something that doesn't want to stop feeling this way.

Maybe that's what being in love feels like.

Or maybe I'm just calling it love because I don't know what else to call it.

Loving her feels like drinking hot, fresh coffee when you're freezing.

It feels like sitting beside a campfire on a snow-covered mountain.

It feels like eating something sweet when you've been craving sugar all day.

It feels like finding water after walking through a desert.

Maybe that's not even love.

Maybe that's just what it feels like when you're deeply in love.

Because suddenly, everything leads to her.

Every thought.

Every word I write.

Every song I listen to.

Every movie I watch.

Every time I look at the sky.

Every morning when I watch the sun rise.

Every evening when I watch it disappear.

The bright orange light hitting my retina.

The warmth I feel from the sunlight.

The reflection I see in the water.

The tree I see while walking to the office.

The cat sitting near the parking lot.

A reel I watched about love.

The picture of Radha and Krishna on my wallpaper.

The birds flying across the sky.

Everything I'm seeing suddenly seems beautiful.

I don't know what changed.

Maybe the world didn't change.

Maybe I did.

After talking to her, everything looks colourful.

Everything feels right.

The way it should be.

The way God created it.

The way everything simply is.

Even my thoughts feel different.

The feelings inside me have started to bloom.

Like a flower.

I guess this is what happens when you're in love.

When you're deeply in love with someone.

You start seeing them everywhere.

Even when they're nowhere.

And somewhere inside all of this, there is still one question.

Will she ever feel the same?

Will she ever understand?

I don't know.

But regardless of that, I just want her to feel special.

The way that flower feels special to me.

I want to protect her.

Like the tree protects the flower.

From the heavy rain.

From the fast winds.

Holding it gently.

Not letting it fall.

Not letting the things that could hurt it get close.

I want to hold her like that.

Gently.

Not controlling her.

Not owning her.

Just protecting her.

And maybe, one day, that tree will understand me.

Maybe it will trust me.

Maybe it will let me take care of that flower.

Maybe one day, it will hand over the responsibility it carried for so long.

And if that day ever comes,

I hope I'll be able to do everything that tree did.

I hope I'll understand everything that flower needs.

I hope I'll be gentle enough.

Strong enough.

Patient enough.

To take care of it.

And if that day never comes...

I hope the flower still blooms.

Even if it isn't in my garden.
