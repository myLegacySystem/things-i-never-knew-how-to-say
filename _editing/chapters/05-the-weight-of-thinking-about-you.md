# The Weight of Thinking About You

I have been feeling more empty with each passing day.

Whenever I talk to her, something inside me gets refilled. Even if it is just a conversation, even if it is about something ordinary, somehow that emptiness becomes quieter. But when the day is quiet, when we don't talk, I feel it again — only this time, it feels even heavier.

For the past two or three days, we haven't talked. We didn't fight. Nothing happened between us. We simply didn't talk.

And somehow, that silence has left a cold feeling in my heart that refuses to go away.

I keep thinking about her.

What might she be doing right now? What might she be thinking? Is she thinking about me too, or am I the only one sitting here thinking about her?

I don't think even a single minute has passed today without her crossing my mind.

And at some point, I started asking myself: *What am I even doing?*

Where did my motivation go?

Where did my discipline go?

Where did the vision go — the one I had for myself, the one where I was supposed to give everything I had, achieve my goals, become the person I wanted to become?

Is this love?

Is this attachment?

What is this?

I don't know.

All I know is that I am thinking about her constantly. Every second seems to carry a thought of her. I don't know what happened to me, and I don't know what will happen to me if she ever leaves.

If she decides to go away.

We are just friends. I know that. And because of that, there is always a possibility that one day she might find someone who loves her more than I do.

Sometimes I wonder what that person will be like.

Is there someone who could love her more than me?

I don't think so.

But there must be someone she will love the way I love her.

Maybe one day she will introduce that person to me.

I wonder what she would tell him about me.

*Who is he? What is he to you?*

Maybe we would both get jealous in our own ways.

Maybe I would think I could love her better than he could.

But then again, maybe that wouldn't matter.

I wonder if she would still keep me in her life when that person enters it.

Or would she slowly disappear from mine?

I don't know.

All I can think about is what she is doing right now.

Will I ever get the chance to be with her?

To actually be beside her?

To be with her forever?

Will that ever happen?

I don't know.

Right now, I don't even feel like I am feeling anything. I am just thinking about her.

Writing, though, feels strangely good.

It feels like I am talking to my own soul — explaining how much I care about her, how much I want her in my life, how much she means to me.

But I know something else too.

If she were suddenly sitting beside me right now, I would probably panic.

I wouldn't know what to say.

I would probably just look at her.

And keep looking at her.

Maybe my eyes would keep wondering how a person could possibly be this beautiful.

Right now, I just can't get over this emotion.

I can't get over her.

And I don't know what will happen if she decides to leave me.

On that day, I don't know what I will feel.

I don't know how I will react.

Maybe I'll cry.

Maybe I won't.

Maybe I'll stay quiet, just like I am today.

Maybe I'll write.

Not for her.

For me.

Because writing feels good.

It feels like putting a heavy weight down from my shoulders, even if only for a few minutes.

Sometimes I wonder what that day will be like.

I wonder if that day will ever come.

And if it does, I wonder who I will become afterward.

But for now, she is still here.

Not beside me.

Inside me.

In my thoughts.

In my heart.

Maybe after writing all of this, I'll ask myself, *Why am I thinking about her so much?*

I can't waste my life.

I can't spend every moment thinking about someone, right?

I have to enjoy myself.

I have to live.

But the strange thing is, even when I try to enjoy myself, somehow she finds her way into those thoughts too.

No matter how much I try to avoid thinking about her, she is always there.

Maybe the only way is to cut all the threads.

Every thread that leads back to her.

Maybe cutting them would hurt even more.

Maybe I would feel emptier than I do now.

Maybe there would be no emotions.

No feelings.

Nothing.

Just an empty void.

Like a black hole in space.

But for now, I can still talk to her.

Not whenever I want, but whenever she is free.

I can still express my feelings — not all of them, but some. The ones I can share without making them a burden on her.

I don't want her to become emotionally attached to me just because I am attached to her.

I want her to love the person she truly wants to love.

With all her heart.

Not attachment.

Not attraction.

Not affection.

Just pure love.

Because she deserves that.

And somewhere deep in my heart, I don't think I deserve to be that person for her.

Maybe because I overthink everything.

Maybe because I am the kind of person who would limit her growth.

Maybe because I would become a hurdle in her way instead of helping her move forward.

I don't even know why I think about all of this.

But then again, I am an overthinker.

I can't stop thinking.

Even when my brain feels completely exhausted.

Even when I get headaches.

Even when I desperately want my mind to be quiet.

It still finds something to think about.

And even if I somehow stop thinking, my brain and my heart find another way to bring her back into my thoughts.

I wish there were a remote control for my brain.

If there were, I would probably give it to my dearest friend.

Or maybe to her.

I'd tell them, *Please. Just turn it off for a while.*

But that's only a wish.

And some wishes are meant to remain incomplete.

It is 5:44 p.m. right now.

Only forty-five minutes before I leave for home.

And here I am, sitting by the window, writing all of this.

Maybe I won't even reach home.

Maybe I'll get into an accident.

Maybe I won't get the chance to talk to her again.

Ever.

And somehow, even in that moment, I think I would still think about her.

I would think about my friends.

My family.

My mom.

My dad.

My sister.

And her.

Maybe for the last time.

Maybe I would leave this world thinking that somehow, through these stupid words, my feelings might reach her.

To someone reading this, these might just be words.

But to me, they are everything I have left.

The person reading this might think:

*How stupid is this guy?*

*What is he even thinking?*

*Why is he acting like this?*

*Is he mad?*

*Why does he think so much?*

But how can I explain to them that I have always been like this?

And maybe I will always be like this.

An overthinker.

Until my last breath.

I am glad I can write all of this.

I am glad that God has given me so much of what I needed.

And I hope He gives me some of what I want too.

But I am grateful for what I already have.

I don't want to be greedy enough to ask for more.

Or maybe I do.

Maybe I'll ask for everyone's happiness.

Maybe I'll ask for everyone's dreams to come true.

Maybe I'll ask for my mom's health.

I want her to be healthy enough to attend my wedding.

Or maybe even my funeral.

She is all I have right now.

Everyone has their mother, but not everyone understands how beautiful that bond really is.

Maybe that is true love.

I have never told my mom how much I love her.

But I have shown her through my actions.

And maybe it is the same with her.

Maybe I have never been able to say everything I feel, but I hope she can see some of it in the way I care.

After my mom, I sometimes wish someone would come into my life with that same kind of love.

Not exactly the same.

Just enough.

Enough love to fill the empty space inside me.

Just true love.

I wouldn't ask anything else from her.

Not a single thing.

Just true love.

As I write these words, my hands are getting tired and red. I don't think I can write much more.

But it isn't really me writing anymore.

It is the feeling inside me forcing me to keep going.

Sitting beside the window.

Watching the sky.

Watching people.

Watching the rain.

It makes me think about how beautiful everything is.

And how much more beautiful life could be if the people you love were sitting beside you.

Sometimes I wonder whether all the things I am saying right now will remain true.

Will I still feel this way if someday I find someone who loves me with all her heart, and I love her with mine?

Will things stay the way they are now?

Or will I become a completely different person?

Will the situation change me?

Will her behaviour change me?

Will loving someone change who I am?

So many questions.

And I don't have an answer to any of them.

I only know one thing.

Right now, I am overthinking.

A lot.

A lot.

A lot.

I still wish there were a switch to turn off overthinking.

It is 5:58 p.m. now.

For today, I'm stopping.

I'm going to scroll through some reels, and then I'm heading home.

Tomorrow is Saturday.

I'll enjoy it.

I'll rest.

I'll try to live without thinking too much.

For now, I'll stop typing this shit.

The person I love has already warned me not to overthink.

And maybe she is right.

But writing this felt good.

To whoever is reading this, please don't judge me.

I'm just writing down the emotions I can't even share with anyone.

Because most people have only seen me smiling.

I'll keep smiling.

I probably always will.

But deep down, this is who I am.

An overthinker.

A person who feels too much.

And a writer who writes because sometimes writing is the only way to put the weight down.

The only way to breathe.
