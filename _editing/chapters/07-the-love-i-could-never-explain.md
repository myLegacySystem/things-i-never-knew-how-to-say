# The Love I Could Never Explain

It is just another random day at work.

People are talking. Phones are ringing. Screens are glowing. Everyone seems busy with their own lives.

And somehow, in the middle of all of it, I am thinking about her again.

I don't even remember when it became this constant.

Not a single second seems to pass without her crossing my mind. Not a single day has gone by without me thinking about her. Sometimes it's something she said. Sometimes it's a memory. Sometimes it's a small thing that reminds me of her.

And sometimes there is no reason at all.

She is simply there.

In my thoughts.

In the quiet moments.

In the spaces between everything else.

And here I am, sitting at work, pretending to be busy with my day while my heart is somewhere else entirely.

With her.

I don't know if she will ever understand how much I love her.

Maybe she never will.

Maybe she will never know how many times I have thought about her when she wasn't there. How many little moments I have replayed in my head. How many imaginary conversations I have had with her. How many futures I have quietly created in my mind without her ever knowing.

Maybe one day she will find these words.

Maybe she will read them and laugh.

Maybe she will feel nothing.

Maybe she will wonder why I felt so deeply.

Maybe she will never even know that these words existed.

And perhaps that's okay.

Because some feelings are so enormous that they don't need an audience.

Sometimes it's enough that *I know*.

I know how much she means to me.

I know how genuinely I have loved her.

I know that I didn't love her because I wanted something from her.

I loved her because somewhere along the way, she became important to my heart.

A mother can feel when her child is hurting without being told. I wish I could somehow make her feel the depth of what exists inside me in that same way.

Not explain it.

Not convince her.

Just let her feel it for one moment.

Because words seem too small for something this big.

She is my first true love.

I have never loved anyone the way I have loved her.

And maybe that's why it feels so different.

There was a warmth in loving her.

A kind of warmth I had never experienced before.

It made ordinary moments feel important.

A conversation.

A laugh.

A shared secret.

A stupid little memory.

A moment of happiness.

A moment of sadness.

The times we cried.

The things we told each other.

The moments when neither of us had to pretend to be okay.

Somewhere inside all of those moments, I built a little world.

An imaginary world.

A world where I could see everything we hadn't lived yet.

The places we hadn't gone.

The memories we hadn't made.

The conversations we hadn't had.

The ordinary mornings.

The difficult nights.

The laughter.

The arguments.

The making up.

The growing old.

A whole life that existed nowhere except inside my imagination.

And somehow, I lived so many moments there.

Moments that never actually happened.

Maybe that's the cruelest part of loving someone.

You can build an entire life with them in your mind while they are living a completely different life in reality.

I know she sees me as a friend.

And maybe that's all I am to her.

I know I cannot force someone to love me.

And I don't want to.

Love shouldn't be forced.

It should happen naturally.

It should be something two people choose.

Something that grows between them without one person having to beg the other to feel it.

So if she doesn't feel what I feel, then I have to accept that.

Even if accepting it hurts.

Even if some part of me keeps wishing things were different.

There were times when I felt jealous too.

I hated hearing her mention other boys' names.

I hated that strange feeling in my chest when I felt like someone else might be getting a part of her that I secretly wished was mine.

And whenever I felt she was hiding something from me, my mind would immediately start wondering why.

Maybe I was wrong sometimes.

Maybe I wasn't.

But I never liked the idea of lies.

I hate lies because trust means too much to me.

If I give someone my trust, I give it completely.

And with her, I gave more than trust.

I gave her pieces of me I don't think anyone else has ever had.

I gave her my honesty.

My vulnerability.

My memories.

My fears.

My happiness.

My sadness.

My faith in her.

My heart.

Everything.

And I only hope that the things we shared will remain somewhere safe inside her heart too.

Because those moments belonged to us.

The conversations.

The tears.

The laughter.

The things we told each other that nobody else needed to know.

I hope she keeps them there.

Not because I want to control what she does with them, but because they were pieces of a world that existed between two people.

Her and me.

And I hope she never breaks the trust I placed in her.

Because if that trust breaks, something inside me will break with it.

Not because she owes me love.

She doesn't.

But because when you show someone your entire heart, you hope they will hold it gently.

I wish she could find someone she truly loves.

And strangely, I mean that.

I hope someday she meets someone who makes her feel what she has never felt before.

Someone who makes her understand how beautiful love can be.

How pure it can be.

How much care can exist inside it.

How much a person can worry about another person's happiness.

I hope that person loves her deeply.

I hope he makes her laugh.

I hope he stays when life becomes difficult.

I hope he understands the parts of her that she doesn't show everyone.

I hope he protects her heart.

I hope they fall deeply in love with each other.

And I hope she is happy.

Because even though I couldn't be the person she chose, I don't want her to go through life without finding the love she deserves.

And maybe, somewhere along the way, I will find mine too.

Not just me.

I hope everyone does.

Every person who has ever sat alone wondering whether they will ever be loved the way they love.

Every person who has given more than they received.

Every person who has loved quietly.

Every person whose heart has been broken by someone they never wanted to lose.

I hope they all find someone.

Because despite all the pain it has caused me, love is still the most beautiful thing I have ever experienced.

It is also the most painful.

Because sometimes loving someone means realizing you may have to let them go.

And letting go of the person you love most can feel like losing an entire world.

Maybe that's what I am grieving.

Not only her.

But the life I imagined with her.

The world I created.

The future that existed inside my head.

The version of us that was never given the chance to exist.

Maybe that feeling will never come back.

And honestly, perhaps I don't want it to.

Because I don't want another person to replace what she gave me.

That warmth was special because *she* was special.

I don't want to erase it.

I want to remember it.

I want to remember what it felt like to love someone for the first time with everything I had.

Even if it hurt.

Even if it wasn't returned in the same way.

Even if someday I have to accept that she was never meant to be mine.

I will still be grateful that, for a while, my heart knew what it was capable of feeling.

And sometimes I wonder...

Maybe there is another world somewhere.

A world where things happened differently.

A world where she and I found each other at the right time.

A world where the love between us was mutual.

Where we lived all those imaginary moments I created.

Where we travelled together.

Where we laughed until our stomachs hurt.

Where we cried and held each other through the difficult days.

Where we grew old together.

Where neither of us ever had to wonder whether the other felt the same.

Maybe in that world, she chooses me.

And maybe I choose her.

Again and again.

Every day.

Without hesitation.

And maybe, in that world, we are deeply, hopelessly, peacefully in love.

So deeply in love that neither of us ever wants to walk away.

Maybe God has another world waiting for me somewhere.

A world where the love I couldn't have here becomes the life I get to live there.

But here, in this world, on this ordinary day at work...

I am just sitting here.

Thinking about her.

Loving her quietly.

Knowing she may never understand how much she meant to me.

And somehow, despite all of it,

I am still grateful.

Because she taught my heart something I had never known before.

*What it feels like to love someone with everything you have.*

And maybe that's enough for this chapter.

Maybe not the ending I wanted.

But perhaps one day, when I look back at this part of my life, I won't only remember the pain.

I'll remember that once, there was a girl who made my ordinary days feel extraordinary.

A girl who became my first true love.

And a boy who, for the first time, discovered just how deeply his heart could love.
