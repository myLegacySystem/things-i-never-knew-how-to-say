# Will She Ever Understand?

There is something I have been thinking about while writing all of this.

Will she ever understand?

Will she ever understand what all of these words actually mean?

Will she read them one day and feel what I was feeling while writing them?

Or will she think this is cringe?

Will she think I am crazy for writing all of this?

Will she wonder, *Why does this guy want me so badly?*

Maybe she will.

And sometimes I am scared of something else too.

What if she actually does become mine?

What if, one day, she says yes?

What if I finally get the person I have wanted for so long?

Will that period of wanting her ever end?

Will I eventually become comfortable?

Will the excitement disappear?

Will the things I am doing now become effortless and boring?

Will there be a day when having her beside me feels normal instead of something I am grateful for every second?

I don't know.

And maybe that is one of the things I am most afraid of.

Because right now, I constantly feel like I need to make sure she is happy.

But will that ever be enough for her?

Will I ever be enough for her?

Can I actually be someone's reason for happiness?

Or will I somehow ruin the things she already has?

That thought scares me.

Because it is easy to imagine loving someone.

It is harder to imagine being responsible for their happiness.

And sometimes I wonder whether I am capable of carrying something like that.

It is so difficult for my heart.

So difficult for my soul.

To hold this feeling.

To conserve it.

To keep it alive.

The feeling of sitting beside her.

The feeling of having her by my side.

The feeling of wanting her to love me.

Wanting her to love me the way I love her.

But then another thought comes.

She probably won't.

Maybe she never will.

Maybe she can't.

Because whatever I feel for her cannot be measured.

It isn't something she can understand just by reading these words.

She can read every sentence I have written.

She can know every thought I have had.

She can know every fear I have ever carried.

And still, she may never understand the actual depth of it.

Because it isn't that simple.

It is the care I have for her.

The things I think about when she isn't even there.

Her future.

Her safety.

Her happiness.

Her life.

I don't just think about these things sometimes.

I think about her.

Only her.

Since the time she came into my life, I have never looked at another girl in a romantic way.

And I don't think I will while she is still somewhere in my life.

Maybe that sounds crazy too.

I don't know.

But it is the truth.

And right now, while I am writing this, I am still checking my phone.

Again.

Did she reply to my good morning message?

Did she wake up?

Maybe she is awake but hasn't checked her phone.

Maybe she checked her phone but not that account.

Maybe she saw the message and decided to reply later.

Maybe.

Maybe.

Maybe.

I use that word so much.

Maybe.

But perhaps every "maybe" is just another form of my overthinking.

Every possibility becomes a story.

I imagine every version of what could be happening.

And somehow, for a few moments, I live inside those possibilities.

But real life doesn't work that way.

Things don't happen exactly as I imagine them.

They never will.

The story inside my head has no limits.

Reality does.

And still, my heart doesn't stop.

It keeps thinking about her.

Only her.

Sometimes I wonder what would happen if she said yes.

If somehow she chose me.

If somehow we stayed together.

Would I be able to make her happy?

Would I be enough?

Would I be able to give her the life she dreams about?

Would I be able to make her feel loved every single day?

Would I be able to give her the life she deserves?

Sometimes I get tears in my eyes just thinking about it.

Because even her saying yes scares me.

Not only because I wonder whether I deserve that yes.

But because I wonder whether I could actually give her everything that comes with it.

And then there is the other answer.

No.

That one hurts for a completely different reason.

Because if she says no, then I won't be there.

Not beside her.

Not in her world in the same way.

I won't be able to protect her.

I won't be able to know how she is doing.

I won't be able to be there in the little moments.

She will still exist in my world.

But I may no longer exist in hers.

And that thought hurts more than I know how to explain.

But for now, we are friends.

And that is enough for me.

At least for now.

I want to be with her while I can.

I want to talk to her while I can.

I want to know about her life while she still lets me be a part of it.

Maybe one day she will find the boy she wants.

Maybe one day someone else will become the person she chooses.

And maybe then, this will end.

Not just the conversations.

Not just the possibility.

The feeling itself.

Maybe I will have to kill it.

And then kill it again when it comes back.

Because I know myself.

It will come back.

It will grow again.

And I will have to keep killing the same emotion until perhaps one day I meet someone who truly loves me.

If that person exists.

I hope that person finds me at the exact right time.

Because if she finds me too late, after I have spent years destroying everything inside myself, I don't know what will be left.

Maybe I will become emotionless.

Maybe I will stop feeling things the way I am supposed to.

And that wouldn't be fair to the person who eventually wants me beside her either.

She would deserve someone who can love her fully.

Not someone who spent so long killing his own feelings that he no longer knows how to feel.

Sometimes I wonder what I have become.

How miserable I have become.

And maybe this is only the beginning.

Maybe if I keep going like this, I won't even be the same person anymore.

But I have found one way to keep myself from becoming completely miserable.

Writing.

Only this.

Putting these thoughts somewhere outside my head.

Writing them down before they grow too large.

Because if I stop writing, I am afraid they will stay inside me.

And then they will hurt.

They will hurt so badly that maybe I won't know how to recover.

So I keep writing.

Maybe these words are useless.

Maybe they have no purpose.

Maybe whoever reads this will laugh at me.

Maybe they will call me crazy.

Maybe they will abuse me for writing something this emotional about someone who isn't even mine.

And maybe they will be right.

Because what use is any of this?

What does anyone get from reading my feelings?

Nothing.

Absolutely nothing.

And honestly, I hope no one finds this.

But if someone does find it, I hope they don't read it like just another chapter.

Because this isn't just another chapter.

This is what I am feeling right now.

Exactly right now.

This is me.

Sitting at this random desk.

Looking out of the window.

Looking at the sky.

Thinking about her.

Writing about her.

Checking my phone.

Waiting for a notification from her.

And somehow every word I write comes back to her.

Every thought.

Every question.

Every fear.

Every hope.

Everything.

And the one question that refuses to leave me is still the same.

Will she ever understand?

Will these words make her understand how much I care about her?

How much I love her?

Will she understand what I am trying to say between all these words?

Will she understand that this isn't about wanting to own her?

It isn't about controlling her.

It isn't about taking her freedom.

It isn't even about getting something from her.

It is simply that somewhere along the way, she became someone my heart started caring about more than I know how to explain.

And now I don't know how to make it stop.

Maybe I don't want it to stop.

Maybe I am just scared of where it will take me.

And still...

I am checking my phone.

Again.

Maybe there will be a notification from her.

Maybe not.

But for some reason, that one small notification still has the power to make my entire day feel different.

And maybe that is the most embarrassing part of all.

That after everything I have written...

After everything I have understood...

After all the responsibilities waiting for me...

After all the thoughts about my career, my family, my future, and the person I want to become...

My heart can still become quiet for just one reason.

Her name appearing on my screen.
